KisanMitra AI is an AI-powered multilingual agricultural decision-support platform designed to help Indian farmers make better farming decisions.

The Challenge

Farmers often need information about crops, diseases, weather, fertilizers and farming decisions from fragmented sources, causing costly delays.

The Solution

KisanMitra AI brings intelligent agricultural assistance, disease diagnostics, weather advisories, and crop recommendations into one unified platform.

Crop Recommendation

Precision matching by soil type, Kharif/Rabi cycle, and water.

Fertilizer Guidance

Weather & Agri Alerts

Live meteorological telemetry with precautionary warnings.

Multilingual Voice AI
